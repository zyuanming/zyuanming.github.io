V2EX-Z Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

V2EX-Z is an independent third-party client for browsing V2EX content on iOS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by V2EX or v2ex.com.

Information We Collect

V2EX-Z does not operate its own backend server and does not collect, sell, or share personal information through a developer-operated backend.

The app may store basic app preferences and session-related data locally on your device so that the app can function, remember settings, and keep you signed in where applicable.

V2EX Account and Website Data

If you sign in to V2EX or browse V2EX content through the app, your interaction is with the V2EX website and service. V2EX-Z acts as a client interface and does not control V2EX accounts, posts, replies, private messages, moderation, or account deletion.

For V2EX account, content, and service-related privacy matters, please refer to the official V2EX website: https://www.v2ex.com.

Third-Party Services

The app may load content, images, web pages, or other resources from V2EX and from external links contained in V2EX content. Those third-party websites and services may have their own privacy practices.

Apple may process purchase, download, crash, diagnostic, and App Store related information according to Apple's own policies.

Analytics and Advertising

V2EX-Z does not use developer-operated advertising tracking and does not sell personal data.

Children

V2EX-Z is not directed to children. The app is intended for users who can access and use the V2EX website according to the rules and policies of that service.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the V2EX-Z app, contact zyuanming@outlook.com.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
